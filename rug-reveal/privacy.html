Rug Reveal privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers Rug Reveal, a mobile game by Hugren Enterprises LLC ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. Rug Reveal has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you, and the game doesn't send any information from your phone.

Your game progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

The game engine

Rug Reveal is made with the Unity engine. Unity's optional usage and diagnostics reporting is turned off in the game, so nothing is sent to Unity Technologies either.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version of the game adds ads, analytics or anything else that collects information, we'll update this policy first and name the companies involved. The date at the top shows the latest change.
